Feature concept for Lagos Life

Besties.

Lagos Life already has friends, chats, companies and rides across the city. Besties ties them together: two players who own businesses together, split the till, and ride through Lagos in the same car.

This is a working prototype. Every number on the phone is computed: ownership, profit splits, votes, fares, needs and travel time.

  1. Ask @ochibu to be your bestieBestie tab. Needs 80 friendship, so the others are locked.
  2. Sell them a stake in Nollyflix, then cash outCompany tab. The till splits by ownership, to the naira.
  3. Propose a new branch and watch the voteSpends of ₦1m or more need both owners.
  4. Ride to Yaba togetherMap tab. Pick a ride, then switch cameras, change the station, gist, and buy gala from a hawker in the Third Mainland go-slow.
Designed and built by Fayosi, frontend and UX developer. Independent concept, not affiliated with the Lagos Life team.
