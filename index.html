Feature concept for Lagos Life

Besties.

It starts in your messages. Chat with a friend three days running and the streak unlocks a company you start together. Going places together is open from day one, and every chat, ride and activity builds trust.

This is a working prototype. Every number on the phone is computed: streaks, trust, ownership, profit splits, fares and travel time.

  1. Message @ochibuPhone tab. You're on a 2-day streak, so one message today makes it 3 and they become your bestie.
  2. Start a company togetherYou each put money in and own it by what you put in, to the basis point.
  3. Link up and ride to QuiloxPick a ride, switch cameras, change the station and gist on the way.
  4. Do things together when you landYou'll see both of you in the room. Every activity together builds trust, and Paddy trust splits cabs.
